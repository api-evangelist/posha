## Core Product: The Posha Kitchen Robot

### What Posha Does
Posha is a countertop cooking robot that:
- **Autonomously cooks complete meals ** from 1,000+ recipes
- **Cooks full meals from raw ingredients
- **Uses built-in camera and AI** to detect cooking stages and adjust in real-time
- **Manages temperature, timing, stirring** and ingredient addition automatically
- **Requires minimal user intervention** - just prep ingredients and select a recipe
- **Produces restaurant-quality results** with consistent taste, texture, and appearance
- **Reduces active cooking time to near zero

### Key Technical Features

#### Culinary Artificial Intelligence
1. **Computer Vision Eyes**
   - Built-in camera that detects cooking stages in real-time
   - Visual recognition of doneness, color, and texture
   - Adjusts cooking parameters based on visual feedback

2. **Intelligent Timing**
   - Automatically adds ingredients at precisely the right moment
   - Optimizes sequence of ingredient addition for flavor and texture
   - Accounts for cooking duration and updates during the cook time

3. **Smart Temperature Control**
   - Automatically adjusts and manages heat settings
   - Maintains optimal temperature for different cooking phases
   - Prevents burning, overcooking, or undercooking

4. **Perfect Stirring & Mixing**
   - Stirs food with the ideal speed and rhythm
   - Consistent mechanical motion for uniform cooking
   - Adapts mixing intensity based on recipe requirements

#### Hardware Specifications
- Integrated cooking vessel with heating element
- Precision temperature sensors
- Automated stirrer mechanism
- Built-in camera system with AI processing
- Companion app connectivity (iOS & Android)
- Compact countertop design
- Easy-to-clean components
